I work where
curiosity gets
useful.

My practice lives between strategy and instinct. I find the sharpest version of an idea, give it a point of view, then build the conditions for it to move through the world.

This is the long way of saying: I like making meaningful things with thoughtful people. The work is part research, part rehearsal, and always a little bit of a leap.
